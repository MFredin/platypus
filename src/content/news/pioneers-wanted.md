Every guild says it's looking for "active, dedicated players." We'd rather tell you what that actually means at Daybreak.

Active means showing up — not every day, not perfectly, but reliably enough that the people flying alongside you can count on it. Dedicated means caring about the outcome of a mission as much as your own progress, and being willing to learn from members who've been out here longer than you have. We're less interested in your kill count on day one than in how you treat the pilot next to you on day thirty.

We're building a roster that spans playstyles: explorers who chart the unknown, strategists who plan the economy, and fighters who hold the line when it matters. What we're not building is a guild where a handful of people carry everyone else. If you're ready to contribute — not just consume — we want to hear your signal.

Recruitment is open now. Head to the Join page to see what the process looks like, and come introduce yourself.
